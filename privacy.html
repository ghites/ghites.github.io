Privacy Policy

Data Collection and Usage

This application accesses the following Google services for the sole use of the application owner:

  • Gmail — reading, drafting, and managing emails
  • Google Calendar — reading and creating calendar events
  • Google Tasks — reading and managing tasks
  • Google Drive — searching and accessing files

No data is collected from or shared with third parties. All data remains on the owner's local machine and within the owner's Google account.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the owner's device. No data is transmitted to external servers.
